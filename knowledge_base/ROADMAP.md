# QEC Frontier Ingestion Roadmap

## Objective

Continuously expand the knowledge base from the initial seed set to a decision-grade corpus without pausing engineering work.

## Scale targets

- 25 sources: first opportunity-ranking refresh
- 50 sources: first saturation and prior-art map
- 100 sources: strategic recommendations should begin stabilizing
- 250 sources: broad frontier coverage
- 500+ sources: long-term intelligence platform

## Source mix

- papers and preprints
- conference proceedings and talks
- GitHub repositories
- patents and applications
- standards and interfaces
- industry reports and technical blogs
- funding and commercial signals

## Review rules

1. Every source must pass schema validation.
2. Every novelty claim must name nearest prior art.
3. Every strategic recommendation must cite at least 10 directly relevant sources once the corpus passes 100 records.
4. Evidence and engineering judgment must remain separate.
5. Findings must update the RevBPGD, decoder-IR, verification, and observability decisions immediately rather than waiting for a final report.

## Recurring outputs

At 25, 50, 100, and every additional 100 sources, generate:

- category counts
- momentum map
- implementation-maturity map
- active GitHub map
- patent-overlap map
- crowded-versus-underexplored assessment
- QR opportunity scorecard
- explicit go/no-go updates
